Our Objects

Education & Skill Development in Drone Technology

To promote, impart, and advance education, skill development, and vocational training in the field of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), drone technology, robotics, and allied emerging technologies.

Training Centres & Certification

Establishing and running training centres, institutes, and workshops to conduct free or subsidized drone pilot training, certification courses, safety programs, and awareness camps for students, unemployed youth, women, farmers, and economically weaker sections of society.

Curriculum & E-Learning

Designing and developing educational curriculum, study material, e-learning modules, and training kits related to drone technology, and making them available to educational institutions at no or nominal cost.

Scholarships & Fellowships

Granting scholarships, stipends, fellowships, and financial assistance to deserving students pursuing education or research in UAV technology.

Seminars & Awareness

Organizing seminars, webinars, conferences, and exposure visits to spread scientific awareness and knowledge about the safe and productive use of drones for public benefit.

Our Services

What We Do

Three pillars that guide every program we run, built around the real needs of the communities we serve.

Education Support

Scholarships, learning materials, and after-school tutoring that keep children in school and on track.

Skill Development

Vocational training for youth and women, equipping them with practical skills for sustainable livelihoods.

Social Welfare

Health camps, nutrition support, and welfare drives that address the needs surrounding a child's education.
